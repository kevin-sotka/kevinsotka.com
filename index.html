01 / The work

Four job titles. One instinct.

These look like four different careers on paper, and in practice they have all been the same move: find the thing nobody has claimed yet, understand it properly, and then do it. Every role below started as work that was not in anyone's job description.

01 / STRATEGY

Business strategy

Reading a business to find where value actually gets created and where it quietly leaks out, then making the case for change to the people who control the budget. Ideas do not move without a number attached.

02 / ENABLEMENT

AI enablement

Built an AI enablement program from scratch inside a global electronics manufacturer, serving six operational functions. Non-technical teams went from curious about AI to pitching their own funded use cases.

03 / PROGRAMS

Program management

Supported an international engineering team as they stood up a state-of-the-art AI and data runtime and harness platform inside a global applied-AI office, coordinating across time zones, languages, and very different levels of technical maturity. Also led training delivery.

04 / OPERATIONS

Operations management

Operational readiness and service delivery at real scale, with real throughput and turnaround targets. This is where I learned that people and process decide whether a change sticks.

03 / How I work

Built to last past the final session.

Most of the difficulty lives in the stretch between finishing a course and shipping something that delivers value, so everything I do is built around getting teams across it. The shape is deliberately repeatable, so it works for one team or for twenty.

PHASE 01

Discovery

Before any workshop I learn where the team actually is. Surveys and light pre-work surface the real pain points, and the material gets rebuilt around what I find. Nothing generic survives this step.

PHASE 02

Workshop

Hands-on rather than lecture. Teams work their own use cases the whole way through, and at the end they pitch them to their own sponsors, the way you would pitch an investor.

PHASE 03

Sustainment

This is where most programs stop and mine keeps going. I track each use case toward development, run office hours to keep people unblocked, and recruit champions inside the team to carry it.

01 / FIND

Find the opportunity

Turn a vague frustration into a concrete problem worth solving.

02 / SHAPE

Shape the solution

Design how AI fits the real workflow, with a human in the loop where it counts.

03 / SOURCE

Build or buy

Decide what to build, what to buy, and how fast you can get to something working.

04 / FUND

Make the business case

Put numbers on it. This is the step that turns a good idea into a funded one.

05 / TEST

Prototype and validate

Put it in front of real users and learn what the slides could not tell you.

06 / PROVE

Measure and improve

Instrument the result so the impact is provable and the next move is obvious.

Agent infrastructure · SodClaw

An orchestration system I run my own work on

A personal agent orchestrator with a persistent context brain, subagent workflows, a tracked portfolio with value and complexity scoring, and a dashboard to see the whole thing at once. My favorite part is the least glamorous: a budget guard that stops a run before it overspends, and a verifier agent that grades the work before anything reaches me.

It is the most honest answer I can give to "do you actually use this stuff." I have been living inside it for a year.
