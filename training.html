01 / What teams learn

From vague frustration to a funded use case.

Every engagement follows the same repeatable arc: discovery before the room, a hands-on workshop where teams work their own use cases end to end, and sustainment after, so the work keeps moving once I leave.

PHASE 01

Discovery

Before any workshop I learn where the team actually is. Surveys and light pre-work surface the real pain points, and the material gets rebuilt around what I find.

PHASE 02

Workshop

Hands-on rather than lecture. Teams work their own use cases the whole way through, and at the end they pitch them to their own sponsors, the way you would pitch an investor.

PHASE 03

Sustainment

This is where most programs stop and mine keeps going. I track each use case toward development and run office hours to keep people unblocked.

02 / Formats

Three ways to bring this to your team.

Every engagement is customized to where your team actually is. These are the shapes it usually takes.

FORMAT 01

Half-day training

A focused session for a team or department that needs a fast, hands-on introduction: finding real use cases in the room and leaving with a first draft of a business case.

FORMAT 02

Cohort workshop

A multi-week enablement program for a team that is ready to build. Teams work their own use cases from discovery through a capstone pitch to their sponsors.

FORMAT 03

Sustained community

Ongoing office hours and a change-champion network for organizations that want the habit to outlast the workshop. This is where adoption actually gets measured.
